Selected work

Three projects, and the decisions they were meant to inform.

Budget impact

What would reimbursing a breast cancer drug cost?

I built a five-year budget impact model for abemaciclib in HR+/HER2− metastatic breast cancer. Reimbursing it would add about VND 55.83 billion in spending, and the sensitivity and scenario analyses showed that drug price and reimbursement rate were the main drivers.

HTARI, 2024–2025

Real-world data

Do patients who take their statins cost less?

Using hospital records of 16,350 outpatients with dyslipidemia, I found that about 30% were adherent and that better adherence went with lower total costs. I built the pipeline in Python and an XGBoost model to forecast future costs.

Undergraduate thesis, 2024

Evidence synthesis

Making cost data from 100+ studies comparable

For a systematic review and cost-effectiveness analysis of dacomitinib, I extracted data from more than 100 articles, converted costs to one currency and designed a standard extraction template for inconsistent, incomplete cost data.
